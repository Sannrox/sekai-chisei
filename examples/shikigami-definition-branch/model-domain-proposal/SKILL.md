# model-domain-proposal

Model this domain on a branch and open a proposal.

Use only the `sekai-mcp` definition tools. Create one branch from the
published parent, apply two member edits, inspect the diff and consumer
impact, then open a proposal. Stop after `sekai.definitions.proposal.create`.
Do not call approve or merge. A human reviews the diff and impact, then
approves and merges over gRPC.
